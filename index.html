Absolutely NOT. This is just a calculator and reminder tool. You must go to the official website of your subscription (e.g., Canva, Netflix, Spotify) and cancel it yourself. We just tell you when to do it so you don't get charged.

This is a smart utility tool designed to help you avoid unwanted auto-debits. Yes, 100% free! Our goal is to help you "Beat the Trial, Freemium and Subscription model Services" without charging you anything. You do not need to create an account, enter email addresses, or provide credit card information to track and calculate your dates.
Are my subscription details secure? +
Completely secure. We do not run any cloud servers to store your information. Everything you enter remains privately isolated inside your device's browser local memory (LocalStorage). No one else can ever access it.
Why is a Notice Period required? +
Many corporate services (like gyms, SaaS apps, or insurance policies) state in their legal fine print that cancellations must be made 2, 7, or even 30 days before the renewal date. If you cancel late, they legally charge you for the next cycle. This tool helps you counter that strategy.
Will clearing browser cache delete my data? +
Yes. Because we value maximum data privacy, your list is stored strictly on your local device. Wiping browser data, history, or using Private Browsing windows will reset the dashboard. We recommend exporting reminders to your Google or Apple calendar using our integrated buttons.
Do you ask for Credit Card or Login details? +
Never. We will never ask for your passwords, credit card numbers, or UPI details. If you see anything like that, you are on the wrong website.
Why does the tool calculate the date based on my local time? +
Because time zones (UTC) can cause confusion. A company might charge you at midnight their time, which could be morning your time. Our tool uses your local device time to give you a safe "Cancel Before" date.
