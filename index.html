Cookie Policy & Data Notice

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how our website uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our platform, measure performance, and deliver personalized content or advertisements.

1. What are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small data files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember user preferences, and provide reporting information.

Local Storage (HTML5) is a web storage mechanism that allows us to store data locally within your browser. Unlike cookies, Local Storage data does not get transmitted to servers on every HTTP request, making it safer, faster, and more private for offline tracking features.

2. Types of Technologies We Use

We utilize the following categories of storage and tracking technologies:

  • Strictly Necessary & LocalStorage (Essential): These are required for the website to function properly. We use browser LocalStorage exclusively to store your calculator settings, subscription entries, and user preferences on your local device.
  • Analytics Cookies: We use services like Google Analytics to collect aggregate information about website traffic, visitor interactions, and usage patterns. This helps us measure performance and improve user experience.
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense): Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

3. How to Control & Manage Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject optional cookies. Here is how you can manage your preferences:

  • Browser Settings: You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though your access to some functionality and areas may be restricted.
  • Managing LocalStorage: Clearing your browser history, cache, or site data will wipe your saved dashboard preferences and entries. Using Private/Incognito mode will automatically wipe LocalStorage once you close the browser session.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Opt-Out Settings

To exercise control over personalized advertising served by third parties, you can utilize the following official tools:

  • Google AdSense Preferences: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-Party Opt-Out: Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
